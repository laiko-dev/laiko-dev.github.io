# AI for Construction FAQ: Straight Answers for Builders and Trades

This page collects the questions builders and trades most often raise when they first look into AI for construction, with short, direct answers. Where an answer depends on your business, it says so.

## What does 'AI for construction' actually mean right now?

For most small and mid-sized building businesses it means software that handles office and communication work: answering calls, writing first drafts, reading documents and sending follow-ups. It is not about robots, and it is not about replacing tradespeople.

## Where should a builder start?

Start with whatever costs you the most time outside working hours, as long as it is repetitive and low-risk. For many businesses that is the phone and the paperwork they are waiting on from other people. Avoid starting with anything that carries your signature.

## Does AI help if I am on site all day?

That is the main reason to use it. Tools you have to sit down and operate do not help much when you are on a roof. Agents that run in the background, such as those laikodev builds to answer calls and chase paperwork 24/7, are designed for people who are not at a desk.

## Will I have to change my job management software?

You should not have to. Look for AI that works with what you already use. laikodev builds its agents around systems like ServiceM8, Tradify, simPRO and Procore so the results land where your team already looks.

## Can AI write my safety documents?

It can write a draft. It cannot verify that the draft matches your site, your crew and the conditions on the day. A competent person must review and sign off a SWMS, JSA or RAMS. Treat AI as the first draft and you save time; treat it as the final version and you take a risk you should not take.

## Can AI read a contract for me?

It can give you a summary and flag clauses that look unusual or one-sided. It is not legal advice, and it cannot weigh those clauses against your appetite for the job. Use it to focus your reading, not to replace it.

## Can AI price my jobs?

Estimating AI can help prepare: summarising scope, drafting inclusions and exclusions, listing questions for the client. It cannot see the site or know your crew. The price stays with you.

## How long does it take to get running?

It depends on the provider and the scope. Narrow workflows can be quick; laikodev agents go live within 7 days of a free intake call. Broad, everything-at-once projects take longer and fail more often.

## Do I need a consultant?

Not always. If you know exactly which task you want handled and your systems are in order, a product may be enough. If you are not sure where your time goes, someone who understands both construction and AI can stop you automating the wrong thing. ## What should never be handed to AI?

Signing safety documents, accepting contract terms, deciding which jobs to take, handling conflict and looking after your people. If a tool claims to take over any of these, be wary.

## Is it worth trying something free first?

Yes, if it lets you test real work. laikodev's free agents for contract review, SWMS, JSA and RAMS let you compare an AI draft with what you would have written yourself, on documents you already understand.
